## Installed dependencies

Setup installs dependencies through pip. The Windows FFmpeg executable supplied by imageio-ffmpeg is GPLv3-or-later; other FFmpeg builds may use different licence options.

Demucs and drumsep model weights download from their upstream projects on first use. They are not included in this repository.
